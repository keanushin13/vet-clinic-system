# Veterinarian — Step‑by‑step Feature Guide

## Overview

Quick reference for veterinarians: calendar, patients, medical records, inventory, messages, schedule, and profile.

## Prerequisites

- Logged-in account with `veterinarian` role.

## Navigation

- Calendar: `/vet` or `/vet-schedule`
- Patients: `/vet-patients`
- Medical records: `/vet-medical-records`
- Inventory: `/vet-inventory`
- Messages: `/vet-messages`
- Notifications: `/vet-notifications`

---

## Calendar — Review & manage appointments

Goal: View your schedule, add notes, update appointment status.

Steps:

1. Open `Vet Schedule` (`/vet` or `/vet-schedule`).
2. Use Month nav to view dates; click an event to open details.
3. Click **Add Appointment** to create a new slot/appointment (pet selection, date/time, reason).
4. Update status (Confirmed / Completed) as you handle visits.

Expected: Appointment times display with pet names, statuses shown by color.

---

## Patients & Medical Records

Goal: Access patient list, add or update medical records.

Steps:

1. Open `Patients` (`/vet-patients`) to search for a patient.
2. Open a patient to view medical history; use `Medical Records` to add entries (diagnosis, meds, notes).
3. Use AI insights (if permitted) to generate health summaries from records.

Expected: New medical record appears in patient history.

---

## Inventory & Messages

- Inventory: update usage/stock levels in `Vet Inventory`.
- Messages: respond to staff/owners via `Vet Messages`.

---

## Verification checklist

- Calendar events are visible and editable.
- Can add medical records and view AI insights.
- Inventory updates persist and messages deliver.

_End of Veterinarian guide._
